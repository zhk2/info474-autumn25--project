Understanding Tariffs

A tariff is a tax imposed by one country on goods and services imported from another country. Tariffs serve multiple strategic purposes: protecting domestic industries from foreign competition, generating government revenue, and addressing trade imbalances.

The US trade policy from 2018-2024 marked a significant shift toward protectionism, with tariffs imposed on hundreds of billions of dollars worth of goods.

The Future of Trade

This scatter plot summarizes three distinct periods: pre-trade war (2015-2017), peak trade tensions (2018-2020), and the recent period (2021-2024).

Each point shows the relationship between tariff tension (measured by the share of positive tariff changes) and trade volatility (year-over-year percentage changes in global trade).

The pattern suggests that higher tariff tension correlates with increased trade volatility—but the future remains uncertain.
