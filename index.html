How it affects consumers?
Tariffs are placed on many different products, specifically on Chinese companies. This can influence industries such as supply-chain, (ex. slowing down production), as well as the technology-sector, which relies heavily on outsourced materials.
Where are we headed?
It's difficult to say. Billions, if not trillions, of dollars go into trying to predict what CEOs, world leaders, and most importantly consumers will do. Global trade, for the most part, has been on an up / down (look at chart) trend.
