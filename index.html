How it affects consumers?
Each line shows how dependent US consumers are on imported goods in a major category, like electronics, chemicals, manufactures, and mineral fuels. Values are normalized so you can compare categories on the same scale. The shaded vertical bands mark years when average US tariffs increased, making it easier to see which types of goods became more exposed during tariff hikes.
Where are we headed?
From 2015 to 2024, global trade in goods grew by roughly forty percent, even with some sharp ups and downs. The solid line in the chart shows those observed values. The shaded region and dashed line simply extend the recent 2019–2024 trend out to 2030 to give a sense of what might happen if conditions stayed similar, but this is only an illustrative straight-line projection, not a full economic forecast.
